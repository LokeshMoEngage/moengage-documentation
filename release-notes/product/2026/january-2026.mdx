## New Enterprise Integrations

MoEngage has now expanded its ecosystem with the following new integrations across sales, support, and data teams to provide a unified source of truth and faster campaign execution:

- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales**: Align sales and marketing by nurturing prospects based on pipeline stages and providing sales teams with visibility into marketing engagement. For more information, refer to [Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales](/partner-guide/data-and-analytics/crm/microsoft-dynamics-365-sales/microsoft-dynamics-365-sales).
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service**: Protect brand reputation by automatically suppressing promotional campaigns for users with open high-priority support tickets. For more information, refer to [Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service](/partner-guide/support-and-service/support/microsoft-dynamics-365-customer-service).
- **Kafka**: Stream high-volume events directly into MoEngage in real-time to trigger immediate, high-reliability campaigns. For more information, refer to [Kafka](/partner-guide/data-and-analytics/workflow-automation/etl/kafka).
- **Adobe Experience Manager (AEM)**: Streamline content velocity by flowing creative assets seamlessly from AEM into MoEngage campaigns. For more information, refer to [Adobe Experience Manager](/partner-guide/personalize-and-optimize/dynamic-content/adobe-experience-manager/syncing-experience-fragments-as-content-blocks).
- **Amazon Connect**: Convert support signals into revenue by triggering retention flows or satisfaction surveys the moment a ticket closes. For more information, refer to [Amazon Connect](/partner-guide/messaging-channels/voice/amazon-connect).
- **Adobe Experience Platform (AEP) \[Paid Add-on\]**: Sync MoEngage engagement data (clicks, opens) back to Adobe’s CDP to power real-time personalization. For more information, refer to [Send MoEngage events to Adobe](/partner-guide/data-and-analytics/cdps/adobe-real-time-cdp/send-moengage-events-to-adobe).
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales (Stream) \[Paid Add-on\]**: Empower sales teams with real-time intent signals by updating them the moment a lead interacts with a marketing campaign. For more  information, refer to [Stream Events to Microsoft D365 Sales](/partner-guide/data-and-analytics/crm/microsoft-dynamics-365-sales/stream-events-to-microsoft-d365-sales).

## New Channel: RCS in Flows

MoEngage now supports Rich Communication Services (RCS) as a native channel within Flows, allowing you to deliver interactive, branded messaging experiences to Android users.

**Key Capabilities:**

- **Rich Campaign Creation**: Build campaigns directly on the flow canvas using custom templates, rich media, and interactive suggestions.
- **Advanced Personalization**: Use attributes like `{{Name}}` to personalize content and Locale settings to deliver messages in the user's preferred language.
- **Testing & Optimization**: Validate messages with Personalized Previews and optimize performance using built-in A/B Testing.
- **Performance Tracking**: Access granular analytics, including unique metrics like Read Rates, Click-Through Rates (CTR), and a detailed Failure Breakdown for troubleshooting.
- **Actionable Flow Logic**: Branch your customer journeys based on real-time RCS events: Sent, Delivered, Read, or Clicked.
- **Reliable Fallbacks**: Ensure reach by using subsequent flow nodes to send fallback messages (like SMS) if an RCS message fails to deliver.

For more information, refer to [Add Actions to Flows](/user-guide/flows-cross-channel-messaging/create/actions-in-flows/add-actions-to-flows).
