MoEngage recommends that you whitelist all the MoEngage IPs listed for your data center under [Data Transfer](/user-guide/settings/account/security/ip-whitelisting-in-moengage#data-transfer). MoEngage connects to your system from these IPs for data imports, data warehouse exports, streams, and connection tests on the App Marketplace. To find your data center, see [Data Centers in MoEngage](/user-guide/data/key-concepts/data-centers-in-moengage).
