{/* Export failure FAQs, shared across every export page. Kept here so the
seven pages cannot drift apart as the failure policy changes. */}

<Accordion title="What does the Replay available label mean?">
  MoEngage is holding data it collected while the export was paused. Hover over the label or open the kebab menu, and then click **Replay paused data**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My export was paused for three weeks. Why can I replay only part of it?">
  MoEngage keeps only the most recent 7 days of paused data, so a three-week pause leaves only the last 7 days to replay.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why did my replay come back as Partially Successful?">
  MoEngage delivered some of the data and not the rest, usually because the destination rejected part of it. Fix the cause and replay again. The next replay sends the whole collection, not only the part that failed.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I run two replays at the same time?">
  No. You can run only one replay per export at a time. Wait for the result email before you request another.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I replayed twice and I see duplicate rows. Why?">
  Every replay sends the whole collection again, because MoEngage does not deduplicate. Deduplicate at your end on the `event_uuid` field, which is unique for each event.
</Accordion>
