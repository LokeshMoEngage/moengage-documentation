Push in Flutter is configured per platform: Firebase credentials and the runtime permission on Android, APNs keys or certificates on iOS. The **Basic** pages cover both, plus the callback you use to handle a notification tap in Dart.

iOS also needs notification extensions for rich media and impression tracking.
