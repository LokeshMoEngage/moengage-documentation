Uninstall tracking helps you understand user churn by capturing uninstall events for your Android app. Enabling this setting populates data on the **Uninstalls** page in MoEngage Analytics, which shows when users uninstall your app. For more information on uninstall analytics, refer to [Uninstall](/user-guide/analyze/analytics/uninstall).

## Prerequisites

Before you enable Android uninstall tracking, ensure that your app handles Android silent pushes. If you [handle push display in your application](/developer-guide/android-sdk/push/advanced/push-display-handled-by-application), confirm that it handles silent pushes as well.

If you see blank notifications with no text or logo in your app, your custom push handling is likely not handling silent pushes. Turn uninstall tracking off until your app handles them correctly.

## Enable Android Uninstall Tracking

Perform the following steps to enable uninstall tracking:

1. On the left navigation menu in the MoEngage dashboard, go to **Settings** \> **Analytics** \> **Uninstall tracking**.
2. On the Uninstall tracking page, turn the **Android Uninstall Tracking** toggle on.  
    <img
      alt="Uninstall tracking settings page with the Android Uninstall Tracking toggle turned on and a Test Android silent push button"
      src="/images/moengage_acfeb0.png"
    />
3. Click **Test Android silent push** to confirm that your app handles silent pushes without displaying them to the user.

## How MoEngage Detects Uninstalls

MoEngage sends silent push notifications daily to every device associated with your users and captures the responses. A device is marked as uninstalled when Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) returns an explicit uninstall error (`Not Registered`).

For other error codes that do not explicitly indicate that the device is unregistered, MoEngage does not mark the device as uninstalled and continues to send silent pushes to it.

If uninstall tracking is turned off on the dashboard, MoEngage does not proactively check for uninstalls by sending silent pushes. Failed delivery attempts of push campaign messages still show up as uninstalls.

<Info>
  Uninstall tracking for iOS was deprecated on August 10, 2023, following significant adoption of iOS 15 and iOS 16, which do not support tracking failed delivery attempts through silent push. Failed delivery attempts of regular push campaign messages still show up as uninstalls for iOS devices.
</Info>

## When an Uninstall Is Detected

The date and time recorded on the **Device UnInstall** event in a user profile is the time MoEngage **detected** that the app was uninstalled, not the time the user uninstalled it. MoEngage cannot determine the actual time of the uninstall.

<Note>
  Uninstall counts in MoEngage can differ from Google's uninstall data. MoEngage tracks uninstalls only when silent push or [real-time uninstall tracking](/developer-guide/components-for-sdk/tracking/real-time-uninstall-tracking) is configured, and detection depends on the callbacks MoEngage receives from FCM. Because of this reliance on FCM, uninstall data can be missing in MoEngage for some days. If your uninstall count shows zero, check that silent push or real-time uninstall tracking is enabled.
</Note>

<Note>
  You may see an anonymous user profile that contains only an uninstall event and no other user information. MoEngage creates this guest profile when it doesn't receive the proper uninstall callback for a user, so that the uninstall event is still recorded. To find the campaign behind it, expand the uninstall event under **Activity info** in the profile, note the **Campaign ID**, and search for it on the dashboard. If that campaign was sent to **All Users**, the user uninstalled the app before the campaign could be delivered.
</Note>
