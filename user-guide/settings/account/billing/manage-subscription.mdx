Your subscription determines the products and channels your team can use to plan and run campaigns in MoEngage. Use the **Billing** settings to activate your subscription, review your current plan, and confirm the products included in your contract. For a comparison of the plans MoEngage offers, refer to [Plans and Pricing](https://www.moengage.com/plans-and-pricing/).

## Activate Your Subscription

Activate your subscription to enable the plan contracted for your workspace, along with the products it includes. Use either of the following options.

### Option A: Welcome Guide

After you sign up, MoEngage displays a welcome prompt.

1. Enter your Subscription Code.
2. Click **Submit**.

![The welcome prompt with the Subscription Code field and the Submit button](/images/moengage_1db7e6.png)

### Option B: Setup Guide

1. In the upper-right corner, click the **Need help** dropdown, and then click **Setup Guide**.
2. Enter your Subscription Code and click **Submit**.

![The Setup Guide with the Subscription Code field and the Submit button](/images/moengage_f54fae.png)

## View Your Current Plan Details

Review your plan details to confirm the features available to your team.

1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Billing**, then select the **Subscription** tab.
2. Click **Compare plans** to compare the plans MoEngage offers, or click **View details** on a specific plan to review its full feature list.

![The Subscription tab showing your current plan with Compare plans and View details options](/images/viewcurrentplan.png)

## View Subscribed Products

The **Subscribed products** section lists the products and add-ons included in your active contract. Review this section to confirm which MoEngage products your team can use and when each subscription term ends.

To view your subscribed products:

1. Click **Settings** > **Billing**, then select the **Subscription** tab.
2. Use the **Search by product name** field to find a specific product.

The table displays the following details for each contracted product:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The name of the product or add-on in your contract. |
| Start date | The date the product's subscription term begins. |
| End date | The date the product's subscription term ends. |

<Info>
  This list shows the products in your active contract. To add a product or to confirm your contract terms, contact your customer success manager.
</Info>

![The Subscribed products table with the search field, download icon, and Name, Start date, and End date columns](/images/subscribedproducts.png)

### Download Subscribed Products

Click the **Download** icon to export your subscribed products list for internal reporting or contract review. The downloaded file includes the following columns:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Date | The date the export was generated. |
| Workspace Name | The workspace the export was generated from. |
| Account Name | The billing account associated with the workspace. |
| Product | The name of the product or add-on in your contract. |
| Subscription Start Date | The date the product's subscription term begins. |
| Subscription End Date | The date the product's subscription term ends. |

### Access Permissions

Viewing and downloading subscribed products requires the same permissions as the Usage page. For more information, refer to [Access Permissions](/user-guide/settings/account/billing/usage-details#access-permissions).

### FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is a product missing from my Subscribed products list?">
    The products that are part of your contract appear in this list. To add a product or to confirm your contract terms, contact your customer success manager.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the Subscribed products list reflect what I am billed for?">
    The Subscribed products list shows the products and add-ons included in your active contract, along with their subscription terms. For a breakdown of your usage against billing metrics, refer to [Usage Details](/user-guide/settings/account/billing/usage-details).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when a product's subscription end date passes?">
    To maintain uninterrupted access, contact your customer success manager before a subscription end date to discuss renewal or changes to your contract.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
