On-site Messaging displays pop-ups, surveys, or lead generation forms directly on your desktop or mobile website, or on your TV app, while a user is actively browsing it. This helps you capture leads, gather feedback, and deliver real-time personalized experiences.

Like In-App Messaging, On-site Messaging is an inbound channel — the message appears within the surface the user is already on, rather than being delivered outside it like Push or Email.

<Note>
  A workspace can have up to 200 active In-App or On-Site Messaging campaigns at a time. To raise this limit, contact Support (additional cost applies based on your MTUs).
</Note>

# Use Cases

On-site Messaging supports a range of real-time, on-page engagement goals, including:

- **Real-time personalization**: Show personalized content to visitors based on their behavior or attributes, without interrupting their session.
- **Lead generation**: Collect email sign-ups or other contact details through on-page forms, including [exit-intent](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/on-site-message/create/exit-intent-technology) forms triggered as a visitor is about to leave.
- **Immediate feedback capture**: Run quick on-page surveys to understand visitor sentiment.
- **Promotions and discounts**: Offer a discount pop-up to first-time visitors, or run a promotion using [no-code gamification templates](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/on-site-message/create/no-code-gamification-templates-osm) such as Spin the Wheel or Scratch Card.
- **TV app engagement**: Deliver personalized messages to users of your [TV application](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/on-site-message/create/create-osm-campaign-for-tv) at key moments.
