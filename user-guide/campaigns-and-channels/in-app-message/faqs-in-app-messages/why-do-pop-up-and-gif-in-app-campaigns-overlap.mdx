---
title: "Why Do Pop-Up and GIF In-App Campaigns Overlap?"
description: "Understand why pop-up and GIF-based in-app nudge campaigns overlap in MoEngage and how to prevent it by adding delays or using separate app screens."
---

## Problem

Both a pop-up message and a GIF-based in-app nudge are displayed simultaneously, even though they were expected to appear separately.

## Cause

- A pop-up in-app message is an intrusive template. A GIF-based in-app message is a nudge, which is a non-intrusive template.
- Because the global minimum delay does not apply to nudges, a GIF-based nudge is not suppressed by it and can display alongside a pop-up that's already showing.
- Therefore, overlap between a pop-up and a GIF-based nudge is an expected outcome when running campaigns with nudges.

## Solution

Perform the following steps:

1. Publish the pop-up and the GIF-based nudge campaign on two different screens.
2. Add a delay to one of the campaigns to prevent them from appearing at the same time.
