MoEngage Inform facilitates a unified messaging infrastructure across multiple channels to build and manage transactional alerts.

# Transactional Alerts

Any time-sensitive transactional content that a brand sends to its customers is a Transactional Alert. The following are examples of transactional alerts:

   - Order/Booking/Purchase confirmations and pertinent updates
   - Shipping/Delivery confirmations and applicable updates
   - Security and account alerts for fraud prevention
   - Password resets
   - OTP (one-time password)/ Verification Code
   - User invitations and shares
   - User inaction related to previous transactions

# Legacy Transactional Messaging Structure

A notification is sent across multiple channels simultaneously, employing a multi-channel transactional messaging structure. To implement such a structure, you would need to:

   1. Build APIs for multiple channels
   2. Maintain these APIs across all channels and vendors
   3. Maintain vendor contracts

## Legacy Structure - Drawbacks

The current Transactional Messaging infrastructure has the following drawbacks:

- Lack of requisite developer bandwidth - Heavy reliance on engineering bandwidth to make even the slightest changes
- Lack of visibility on alert (message) performance - Absence of a centralized dashboard to manage alerts, content templates, and delivery partners
- Lack of control over the content - Product, content, and marketing teams have limited control over the content and alert logic because of the wait for engineering bandwidth.

# MoEngage Inform

MoEngage Inform enables brands to build and manage transactional alerts across channels through One API. You can build, iterate quickly, and scale effortlessly.

## Advantages

With MoEngage Inform, brands can utilize a single dashboard without the need to alter or deploy code. It helps to: 

   - send personalized Transactional Messages on multiple channels
   - add new channels
   - build and edit templates in minutes
   - switch seamlessly between messaging vendors
   - debug in real time easily with end-to-end logs
   - collaborate with the product, growth, and marketing teams to analyze and optimize alerts

# Limits and Behavior

Use this section to look up Inform's throughput, tracking, fallback, content, and network behavior. Inform inherits the delivery behavior of the underlying channel (SMS, WhatsApp, Email, Push) for most cases — this section calls out only the Inform-specific details.

## Throughput (RPM)

Inform's throughput is defined by your contract. The default is **10,000 requests per minute (RPM)** and can be increased or reduced based on your contract terms.

- Throughput is **channel-agnostic** — the same RPM limit applies whether the alert is sent through SMS, WhatsApp, Email, or Push.
- If a single Inform API call targets multiple channels, it counts as **one request** but produces **multiple sends**, one per channel.

To change your throughput limit, contact your MoEngage Customer Success Manager (CSM) or the Support team.

## Impression Tracking

Impression tracking for Inform alerts behaves the same as regular Push campaigns. There is no Inform-specific difference. For details, refer to [Push Analytics and Info](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/mobile-push/analyze/push-analytics-and-info).

## Fallback Behavior

Inform supports two fallback mechanisms configured through advanced concepts:

- **Sender fallback** — Automatically select a sender based on user or API attributes. If none of your conditions match, Inform uses the fallback sender you set. Refer to [Dynamic Sender Routing](/user-guide/inform/advanced-concepts/dynamic-sender-routing).
- **Template fallback** — Route the alert to a fallback template when template-selection conditions are not met. Refer to [Dynamic Template Selector](/user-guide/inform/advanced-concepts/dynamic-template-selector).

## Subject Line and Content Limits

Inform does not apply channel-level content limits beyond what already applies to the underlying channel. Follow the limits documented for the channel you are sending on — for example, WhatsApp templates enforce a 1,024-character body limit (refer to [Common Errors in WhatsApp Campaigns](/user-guide/campaigns-and-channels/whatsapp/troubleshooting-and-faqs-whatsapp/common-errors-in-whatsapp-campaigns)).

## IP Whitelisting

IP whitelisting for Inform is **not enabled by default**. If your organization requires MoEngage IPs to be added to your allowlist, contact your MoEngage CSM or the Support team to request whitelisting for your workspace.
