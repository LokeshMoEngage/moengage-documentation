## Overview

MoEngage's Merlin AI suite is built to empower marketing teams through secure, high-performing AI features, including native agents, custom agents, and predictive decisioning.

Our commitment is to maintain high availability and enterprise-grade security for content generation and workflow automation. Security is an inherent part of our identity and informs every architectural decision, including data collection, processing limits, and sub-processor management. Merlin AI guides generative execution towards safety and compliance by combining proprietary algorithms with industry-leading foundation AI models.

## MoEngage Architecture and System Design

We adhere to strict internal AI usage baselines. Customer data is kept isolated from model providers, ensuring sensitive information is never exposed or co-mingled.

Merlin AI operates through a dual, asynchronous system design:

- **Learning from client data (proprietary algorithms):** MoEngage's proprietary algorithms analyze past campaign performance, engagement results, user actions, and workspace-level preferences within a customer's workspace. This system operates without generative AI. For instance, the Merlin AI copywriter uses algorithms like Keyword Impact Quotient (KIQ) to glean insights from historically effective workspace keywords.
- **Foundational generative AI models:** For generative capabilities, Merlin AI integrates with vetted third-party model providers. Output suggestions may leverage insights derived from MoEngage's workspace algorithms. To preserve confidentiality, no personal data, account credentials, or identifiable business details are shared with third-party model providers.

## Key Data Safeguards and Technical Controls

- **Workspace and role-based isolation:** Access is restricted to authorized personnel using role-based access controls (RBAC) and workspace-level isolation to prevent unauthorized campaign creation or data access.
- **No model training on customer data:** Your prompts (inputs), completions (outputs), embeddings, and workspace data are:
  - **NOT** available to other customers.
  - **NOT** used to train or improve third-party foundation models.
  - **NOT** used to improve any third-party products or services.
- **Isolated hosting:** Third-party model integrations (such as Azure OpenAI Service) run in managed, enterprise-tier cloud environments. These instances do not interact with public API services or unmanaged consumer-facing tools.
- **Built-in security and filtering:** Input and output content filtering mechanisms detect harmful content, prevent prompt injection, and reduce biased or safety-violating responses.

## AI Partners and Infrastructure Providers

MoEngage collaborates with enterprise cloud and AI infrastructure providers to enforce multi-layered privacy controls:

- **Amazon Web Services (AWS):** Provides network isolation and secure workspace learning environments for localized data points (such as keyword analytics).
- **Microsoft Azure (Azure OpenAI Service):** Deploys multi-layered security checks, input/output content filtering, and responsible AI safety standards directly within model execution layers.
- **Stability AI:** Deploys prompt screening filters, safety mechanisms, and content authenticity standards for media and creative workflows.
- **Anthropic:** Serves as the foundation for Custom Agents via our enterprise Claude Managed Agents (CMA), delivering isolated runtime sandboxes, multi-layered execution safeguards, and zero model training on customer sessions, files, or memories.
- **Google Cloud (Google Gemini):** Enforces multi-layered security checks and enterprise data privacy protections, ensuring model queries are processed within secure cloud boundaries without training foundational models on your data.

## Shared Responsibility and Human Oversight

While MoEngage implements robust technical and organizational security controls, appropriate use of generated output remains a shared responsibility:

- **Human-in-the-loop empowerment:** Generative AI acts as a high-velocity copilot designed to supercharge your team's productivity, handling heavy lifting like drafting copy, generating creative variations, and structuring complex workflows in seconds. Marketers maintain complete strategic direction and creative control, ensuring every AI-assisted message, creative asset, and campaign is reviewed and approved by your team before going live to your audience.
- **Custom agents and external connectors:** When configuring custom AI agents or connecting third-party applications, customers are responsible for ensuring that external data transfers comply with their internal security guidelines and statutory requirements.
- **Permitted internal business use:** In accordance with the MoEngage Master Subscription Agreement (MSA), Merlin AI capabilities are licensed strictly for the customer's own internal business operations and marketing workflows. Customers may not commercially exploit, monetize, resell, or offer Merlin AI features as a standalone service to third parties unless expressly authorized under a separate Partner or Reseller Agreement.
