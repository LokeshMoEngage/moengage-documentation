Everything you need to know about how MoEngage builds, governs, and secures AI across our platform, including Merlin AI Agents, features, and our predictive and generative capabilities.

## 1. AI Overview and Use Cases

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1.1 What AI capabilities does MoEngage offer?">
    MoEngage incorporates advanced AI and machine learning across predictive segmentation, behavior forecasting, and intelligent journey optimization—including real-time offer decisioning and campaign decisioning. Our platform also features automated anomaly detection alongside generative and agentic capabilities (Merlin AI) for automated copywriting, image generation, segment discovery, and creating and managing autonomous agents. Together, these capabilities enable hyper-personalized, cross-channel customer engagement at scale by automating insights, decisioning, and content creation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="1.2 What is Merlin AI?">
    Merlin AI is MoEngage's suite of AI agents that guide marketing strategy across productivity, content creation, and strategic optimization. It uses a hybrid architecture combining third-party foundation models for content generation with proprietary, in-house models for optimization and prediction. Users provide prompts, and the models return "completions" — but for generative features, the final decision always rests with the marketer. AI outputs are suggestions that must be reviewed and approved by a human before any live campaign deployment. Merlin AI features are provisioned and enabled by default based on the customer's subscribed plan tier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="1.3 Is MoEngage AI generative, predictive, or agentic?">
    MoEngage uses a combination of all three, depending on the feature:

    - **Predictive AI (e.g., Predictive Segments):** Deterministic/traditional machine learning, using Gradient Boosting Classifiers to detect behavioral patterns and assign probability scores for future user actions.
    - **Generative AI (e.g., AI-Suggested Segmentation):** A stochastic model, using a fine-tuned model paired with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to translate natural-language queries into segmentation logic.
    - **Agentic AI:** Purpose-built AI agents (Decisioning, Predictive, Content, Channel) that reason, learn, and make context-aware recommendations based on business objectives, collaborating to deliver personalized experiences at scale — always within defined guardrails and human oversight.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="1.4 What is the business purpose of MoEngage's AI features?">
    MoEngage's AI analyzes customer behavior, preferences, and engagement patterns to deliver hyper-personalized experiences across channels. It personalizes content, optimizes send times and channel delivery, and predicts key outcomes like churn risk to maximize engagement and retention. Additionally, it streamlines and automates marketing operations by assisting teams with campaign management, automated audience segmentation, and deep analytical insights.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="1.5 Can our teams turn AI features on or off? What happens if we do?">
    Certain AI capabilities are enabled by default depending on your subscription plan. While self-serve toggle controls are not directly exposed in the platform settings, our team can disable specific AI features for your account upon request. If you choose to opt out, those AI-powered functionalities will be deactivated for your organization. To request disabling any AI features, please contact your MoEngage account team or Customer Success Manager (CSM).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="1.6 Does the AI app integrate with other enterprise systems?">
    Yes. Integration methods include API connections, SDKs, S3/SFTP transfers, MCP (Model Context Protocol) Connectors, and data warehouse integrations with Snowflake, Amazon Redshift, Microsoft Azure Blob, Google BigQuery, and Databricks. The platform also connects with CRM systems like Salesforce and HubSpot, with real-time synchronization to keep customer profiles current.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="1.7 Can the AI trigger automated actions?">
    Yes, within defined limits. For example, our intelligent delay optimization feature analyzes open rates in real time and automatically adjusts campaign trigger timing based on AI-driven insights. The AI cannot initiate autonomous financial or operational transactions, and any high-impact or irreversible action requires explicit human confirmation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="1.8 What are the permitted business uses and licensing restrictions for Merlin AI?">
    In accordance with the MoEngage Master Subscription Agreement (MSA) and our [AI Terms of Usage Policy](https://www.moengage.com/ai-terms-of-usage-policy/), Merlin AI capabilities are licensed strictly for the customer's own internal business operations and marketing workflows. Customers may not commercially exploit, monetize, sublicense, resell, or offer Merlin AI features—or underlying model outputs—as a standalone service to third parties unless expressly authorized under a separate Partner or Reseller Agreement.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 2. Which AI Models Do You Use, and Where Are They Hosted?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="2.1 Is Merlin AI powered by proprietary models, third-party models, or both?">
    Both. Merlin AI is a hybrid system:

    - **Proprietary models (e.g., Gradient Boosting, Prophet):** Hosted entirely within MoEngage and power core optimization, prediction, recommendations, offer/campaign decisioning, and anomaly detection.
    - **Generative features:** We leverage multiple models across leading enterprise AI providers—including OpenAI models (via Microsoft Azure), Gemini models (via Google Cloud), Claude models (via Anthropic & AWS Bedrock), and Stability AI. As AI technology evolves, we continuously integrate and update to the latest model releases from each provider to deliver optimal performance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.2 Which model/provider(s) are specifically used for inference in Merlin AI Agents?">
    Inference for Merlin AI Agents is powered exclusively by the following model providers:

    - Claude models via Anthropic and AWS Bedrock
    - OpenAI models via Microsoft Azure (Azure Direct Models & Azure OpenAI)
    - Gemini models via Google Cloud

    <Note>
    For other ML-powered Merlin AI features like Predictions, Best Time to Send, Most Preferred Channel, etc., we utilize proprietary ML models hosted directly within MoEngage AWS cloud environments.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.3 Am I able to choose or control which models are used for the AI features in my MoEngage account?">
    Currently, Merlin AI features use models selected and managed by MoEngage, and customers can't switch to a different model or provider within those features.

    That said, we offer an MCP integration that gives customers more flexibility. With MCP, you can connect your own MCP-compatible application (such as Claude and more) directly to MoEngage. Once connected, your chosen model can work with your MoEngage data to power private, in-context workflows, all running on infrastructure you control.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.4 Does inference run via first-party API directly or via third-party providers?">
    Inference for Merlin AI agents currently runs through both pathways:

    - **First-party API integrations:** Direct platform integration with Anthropic models and Gemini / Google Cloud.
    - **Third-party hosted environments:** AWS Bedrock and Microsoft Azure, where Anthropic and OpenAI models are securely hosted in enterprise cloud environments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.5 Where do AI inference and related data processing take place geographically?">
    While core MoEngage customer workspaces and primary customer databases remain securely hosted in their respective regional data centers—such as Frankfurt for EU customers and Mumbai for India customers—all AI inference, processing activities, and custom agent runtimes (including session context and memory under our Anthropic CMA) are currently conducted within the United States (US).

    - **Data Isolation & In-Transit Protection:** Data passed to AI models is transmitted via encrypted channels (TLS 1.3) solely for real-time inference and is processed under enterprise-grade zero-training agreements.
    - **Cross-Border Transfer Safeguards:** For customers hosted in the EU, India, or other local jurisdictions, cross-border data transfers required for AI capabilities are governed by robust transfer mechanisms, including our Data Processing Addendum (DPA) and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
    - **Granular Enablement:** Organizations subject to strict in-country data residency mandates can request disabling US-processed AI agent capabilities without impacting their core regional platform functionality.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.6 Do you have enterprise-grade agreements with these model providers?">
    Yes. MoEngage accesses third-party models via enterprise-grade, secure environments on AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. AWS Bedrock does not store or log prompts. Google offers a zero-data-retention configuration where eligible, and Microsoft Azure offers a modified abuse-monitoring process for enterprise customers. We partner closely with Anthropic (Foundation for Custom Agents) — both via direct enterprise commitments and AWS Bedrock — as the primary foundation powering MoEngage's custom AI agents. These agreements enforce enterprise data protection standards: customer data and agent instructions are never used to train base models, and prompts/completions are processed under strict privacy boundaries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.7 Where is MoEngage's AI infrastructure hosted?">
    MoEngage's AI workloads run exclusively in secure cloud environments—primarily AWS, with additional AI/ML services on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). MoEngage does not host AI on customer premises or in company-owned data centers. Primary cloud regions include Frankfurt (Germany), Virginia and Ohio (USA), Mumbai (India), Singapore, Jakarta (Indonesia), and GCP in the UAE.

    For Custom AI Agents, underlying agent data—including execution sessions, context windows, and agent memories—is hosted and managed within Anthropic's Claude Managed Agent (CMA) infrastructure under MoEngage's enterprise agreement with Anthropic. This setup ensures enterprise-grade data isolation, rigorous security controls, and strict contractual guarantees that customer data is never used to train public models.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.8 Where do our uploaded files and session data live?">
    Custom Agents execute on Anthropic's Claude Managed Agent (CMA) runtime infrastructure. During an active run, each agent operates within a dedicated, workspace-scoped environment that manages uploaded reference assets (e.g., brand guidelines, tone frameworks, or localization glossaries), runtime-generated artifacts, and the agent's persistent working memory.

    Because these capabilities rely on this managed runtime, the session environment—including associated files and contextual memory—is hosted directly within Anthropic's Claude Managed Agent (CMA) infrastructure under our enterprise agreement with Anthropic, rather than inside MoEngage's standard AWS infrastructure.

    **Key Security & Data Governance Controls:**

    - **Sandboxed Tenant Isolation:** Each run executes inside an isolated runtime container mapped strictly to your workspace namespace. Cross-tenant data leakage is prevented by design.
    - **Zero Model Training:** Anthropic is bound by strict enterprise contractual commitments prohibiting the use of your files, prompts, generated outputs, or memory data to train or fine-tune public foundation models.
    - **Data Lifecycle & Retention:** Session-level ephemeral artifacts and execution states are retained only for the lifecycle of the active run or transient operational windows, while workspace-scoped reference files and persistent memory context remain secured under Anthropic's managed enterprise retention boundaries and cannot be cleared or overwritten.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.9 What are the hardware/software requirements to use MoEngage's AI features?">
    None beyond a modern web browser and internet access. MoEngage is a cloud-based, platform-agnostic SaaS product; integration is achieved through lightweight SDKs or APIs added to your existing application or website stack.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2.10 How scalable is the platform?">
    MoEngage's AI platform is horizontally and vertically scalable — processing data from over 1 billion users monthly, handling 100M+ profiles and 100+ concurrent campaigns without degradation, and maintaining SLAs up to 99.99% even during 2x traffic spikes, backed by AWS auto-scaling and redundant data centers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 3. Data Privacy, Context and Model Training

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="3.1 What are the potential inputs and outputs for MoEngage's Merlin AI features?">
    MoEngage has embedded AI-enhanced functionality throughout the platform to help customers streamline workflows and accelerate analysis. Because these AI capabilities operate across the product, any data that a customer has submitted to its MoEngage account could potentially be included as input to, or output from, an AI feature, depending on how the customer chooses to use the feature.

    However, it is important to note that MoEngage's AI partners never use customer data to train their models, and no customer data is retained by any AI partner outside of MoEngage's secure AWS environment except for transient processing, vendor abuse-monitoring obligations (such as automated safety and compliance screening) governed under our enterprise agreements, and, for Custom Agents, the session environment and agent memory hosted with Anthropic as described in Sections 2.7 and 2.8. Moreover, subject to the restrictions on sensitive data outlined in MoEngage's [AI Terms of Usage Policy](https://www.moengage.com/ai-terms-of-usage-policy/), customers remain in full control of determining what data they choose to submit to MoEngage services, and aggregated data can be accessed by Merlin AI agents based on the user's request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3.2 What data may be sent to the model as part of inference?">
    Merlin AI Agents pull context from assets that already live in your MoEngage account (e.g., campaigns, segments, templates, analytics charts, reports) using Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools to answer questions and execute tasks.

    - **Workspace-level Isolation:** Each agent execution operates within a secure, dedicated sandbox environment at the workspace level, ensuring strict tenant boundaries and data segregation for every task.
    - **Data Minimization:** Agents use only the minimum context needed for each task and keep that context scoped to your organization's tenant and the user's role.
    - **PII Protection:** Most MoEngage MCP tools do not access or respond with end-user data. While a few user-level MCP tools (e.g., event logs for a given user) query user data to run analytical tasks, they are strictly designed not to respond with out-of-the-box PII attributes like Email and Mobile Number, or any attribute designated as PII within MoEngage Data Management.
    - **Upcoming Controls:** We are also introducing workspace-level controls allowing administrators to disable specific MCP tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3.3 What does the agent remember, and who can see it?">
    Custom Agents retain operational working memory across sessions to improve consistency on repetitive tasks—such as recognizing your campaign naming taxonomy, preferred report layouts, or brand tone guidelines.

    **Scope & Data Isolation:**

    - **Tenant-Level Isolation:** Agent memory is strictly scoped to your organization's dedicated workspace namespace. Memory stores never cross tenant boundaries, ensuring one customer's agent data is never accessible to another.
    - **Shared Workspace Store:** Within a single workspace, memory is shared across all agents and users, rather than partitioned per agent or individual user. Context captured by one agent is readable by other agents operating in that workspace, accumulating across different team members' runs.
    - **Management & Lifecycle:** Memory cannot currently be inspected, edited, or cleared directly via the UI or APIs. Deleting an individual agent does not purge the shared operational memory it established.
    - **Data Classification:** Agent memory is designed exclusively for procedural and organizational working knowledge (operational conventions, formatting preferences, workflow context)—not for end-user personal data (PII).

    **Governance Guidance:**

    Plan your operational boundaries at the workspace level, not the individual agent level. Because all agents inside the same workspace tap into the same shared memory foundation, isolating operational context requires provisioning separate workspaces.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3.4 Is any customer data, or PII retained, logged, or used for training at any point?">
    - **Model Training:** Our agreements with foundational model providers strictly prohibit the use of customer or end-user data to train, fine-tune, or improve their AI models.
    - **Third-Party Abuse Logging & Zero Data Retention (ZDR):** Certain third-party LLM providers may temporarily retain prompt logs strictly for automated abuse monitoring. MoEngage is actively executing Zero Data Retention (ZDR) agreements across all LLM providers powering Merlin AI.
    - **Agent Sessions & Memory Retention:** Because custom agent sessions are designed to be resumed over extended periods, a fixed auto-deletion retention policy has not yet been enforced on custom agent sessions or memories; however, any session with no interaction for 30 days automatically transitions to a read-only state.
    - **Internal Session Logs:** Internal agent session logs are maintained strictly for debugging, analytics, operational monitoring, and billing purposes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3.5 Will our data be used to train, fine-tune, or improve AI models for other customers?">
    No. Customer personal data is never used to train or fine-tune third-party foundation models. Prompts and completions are not shared with other customers, nor are they used to train third-party models. Learnings from one customer never train algorithms for another. Proprietary core models are trained exclusively on that customer's own data in an isolated environment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3.6 Is our data anonymized or pseudonymized before it's used by AI models?">
    Yes. MoEngage supports PII masking and tokenization across AI/ML workflows, minimizing the use of raw personal data. For third-party generative models, prompts are structured to avoid including personal data unless explicitly required by the workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3.7 Is our data logically and physically segregated from other customers?">
    Yes, customer data and agent operations are strictly isolated at the workspace level. Data security is enforced through role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end encryption, data masking, and tokenization. Dedicated per-customer environments are maintained for models such as AWS Personalize and SageMaker where applicable, ensuring client data is never shared or reused across tenants.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 4. Human Oversight and Control

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="4.1 Do AI outputs require a human review before they go live?">
    Yes. All AI outputs must undergo meaningful human review by qualified reviewers from your team against established criteria before any downstream use. This includes systematic evaluation, collaborative feedback, and documented rationale for any reviewer overrides.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="4.2 What human-oversight model applies — human-in, on, or out-of-the-loop?">
    MoEngage applies a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) model: marketers manually review and approve AI-generated suggestions before implementation. Use cases affecting customer rights, service access, pricing, or account status require mandatory human approval, as do major model changes. Lower-impact use cases (e.g., send-time optimization, copy suggestions) can operate within existing guardrails without per-item approval.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="4.3 Can AI decisions be challenged, reviewed, or appealed?">
    Yes. Customers can request review, challenge, or escalation of AI-driven outcomes through their Customer Success Manager or a support ticket, following a formal escalation path up to executive sponsors if needed. Certain limitations are detailed in our [AI Terms of Usage Policy](https://www.moengage.com/ai-terms-of-usage-policy/).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="4.4 Can autonomous (&quot;agentic&quot;) AI initiate financial or irreversible actions on its own?">
    No. Merlin AI acts strictly as an assistive agent, operating within the authenticated session and permission scope of the signed-in user — it cannot bypass or elevate a user's access rights. Certain high-impact or permanent actions require explicit human confirmation before execution. Guardrails, including a read-only "Controls" card showing key operating parameters, give marketers clear oversight of agent behavior.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="4.5 How are customers informed when AI is involved in a decision or interaction?">
    MoEngage places a disclaimer banner on the customer dashboard to indicate when AI is being used, providing reasoning and auditability so our customers can, in turn, transparently communicate AI involvement to their own end users through their own UI, consents, and policies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="4.6 Who can create agents, who can use them, and who can publish or modify them?">
    Custom Agents follow an ownership-based governance model rather than a role-based permissions matrix. Lifecycle actions (editing, publishing, deleting) are strictly restricted to the agent's creator, while execution relies on the individual user's existing workspace permissions.

    **Creation & Draft Phase:**

    Any workspace member with standard creation permissions can initiate an agent. Agents begin as private drafts, visible and accessible only to their author.

    **Execution & User Access (Least Privilege Enforced):**

    - **Public vs. Private:** Public agents can be run or duplicated by any workspace member; private agents remain exclusive to the creator.
    - **Permission Boundaries:** Running a live agent does not grant privilege escalation. The agent executes within the caller's active authenticated session—two teammates running the same agent will see results strictly constrained to their own dashboard access rights.
    - **Scheduled Executions:** Automated or scheduled runs execute against the permissions and credentials of the user who configured the schedule.

    **Lifecycle Management (Creator-Only Control):**

    - **Modifying, Pausing & Deleting:** Only the designated creator can edit, publish, pause, or remove an agent. Other members cannot alter or delete someone else's agent, regardless of organizational seniority.
    - **Duplication for Collaboration:** To build upon another member's work without risking unintended changes, users can duplicate a public agent into their own roster as a new, independently owned instance.
    - **Policy Validation Safeguards:** Before an agent is published or executed during tests, its system instructions undergo automated backend policy validation to prevent non-compliant behavior.
    - **Memory Persistence:** Deleting an agent removes the agent configuration but does not erase its historical contributions from the shared workspace memory pool.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="4.7 What is the mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard in place for Custom Agents?">
    No Custom Agent can independently publish or dispatch campaigns. Outbound campaign launches and live customer communications remain strictly human-driven actions requiring manual confirmation in the MoEngage dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 5. Bias, Fairness and Explainability

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="5.1 How does MoEngage monitor for bias in AI-generated content?">
    MoEngage monitors bias through rigorous testing, regular audits, and continuous performance monitoring, using diverse training data and human review processes to detect and address unfair bias and ensure fairness and inclusivity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="5.2 Are AI decisions explainable?">
    Yes. MoEngage treats all AI outputs as explainable suggestions rather than 'black-box' decisions. For predictive AI, we provide transparent performance metrics like accuracy, precision, recall, and F-score, alongside plain-language insights on analytics dashboards that detail the underlying behavioral signals and propensity scores. For Generative AI, we ensure complete explainability and auditability by maintaining detailed logs of all model interactions—capturing inputs, prompts, context, and outputs. Additionally, we maintain documented AI model cards across our capabilities.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="5.3 How are hallucinations or unsafe AI outputs controlled?">
    Through data-backed citations, confidence scores, human-in-the-loop review, and configurable exclusion rules for sensitive content and recommendations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="5.4 What guardrails exist for generative AI prompts?">
    MoEngage's generative features are protected by AWS Bedrock Guardrails (prompt sanitization, sensitive-topic classification, prompt-injection detection), along with platform-level controls such as frequency caps, delivery controls, and role-based approvals. Prompt inputs are validated before being sent to AI models, and the system is instructed to decline requests outside its defined role.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 6. AI Governance, Risk and Regulatory Compliance

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="6.1 Does MoEngage have a formal AI governance program?">
    Yes. Our AI governance program is led by the CTO and overseen by the Compliance & Risk Committees, aligned with ISO 42001 and ISO 23894 (AI risk management). It includes formal risk management, monitoring, and incident-response processes, and is supported by our Responsible AI policies covering ethics, bias mitigation, lifecycle management, and data privacy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="6.2 Is MoEngage's AI classified as &quot;high-risk&quot; or &quot;prohibited&quot; under the EU AI Act?">
    MoEngage's AI systems fall within the general scope of "AI systems" under the EU AI Act (e.g., our AI algorithms, the Sherpa AI engine, and AI chatbots) but do not use any prohibited technique under the Act — including subliminal manipulation, exploitation of vulnerabilities, social scoring, emotion inference in workplaces/schools, or untargeted facial-recognition scraping. We continue to perform rigorous self-assessments to track "high-risk" classification questions as the regulation evolves.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="6.3 Which frameworks and certifications support your Responsible AI program?">
    MoEngage aligns with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO 23894:2023, and holds ISO 27001, ISO 27701, and SOC 2 Type II certifications, with an active ISO 42001 certification audit in progress. We also maintain ISO 22301 (business continuity), HIPAA reporting, and CSA STAR.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="6.4 How do you comply with region-specific regulations (GDPR, CCPA, PDPA, DPDP Act, etc.)?">
    MoEngage's AI systems align with GDPR, CCPA, Singapore's PDPA, and India's DPDP Act, including requirements around consent, data subject access/correction rights, and data residency. We maintain a legal register tracking global regulatory obligations and conduct regular internal audits to confirm ongoing alignment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="6.5 Do you conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)?">
    Yes, where required. MoEngage conducts AI Impact Assessments for all AI systems and features, and a full DPIA where the risk profile calls for one — as part of our broader AI governance program's risk-assessment process.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 7. Security and Infrastructure

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="7.1 What encryption standards protect data in transit and at rest?">
    Data is secured with TLS 1.2+ in transit and AES-256 at rest, with encryption keys managed via AWS KMS.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="7.2 How are API endpoints secured?">
    MoEngage API endpoints use Basic Authentication with a Base64-encoded Workspace ID and Data API Key, plus mutual TLS (mTLS) for zero-trust, certificate-based authentication.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="7.3 What authentication mechanisms are supported?">
    MoEngage supports password login, Google Sign-In, and Single Sign-On (SSO). Administrators can restrict login to Google or SSO only, and enable two-factor authentication (2FA) for additional security.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="7.4 Is role-based access control (RBAC) implemented?">
    Yes, with roles including Admin, Manager, Marketer, Developer, and Analyst. Administrators can configure permissions per role or create custom roles with tailored access rights.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="7.5 How do you protect against adversarial attacks, prompt injection, and jailbreaking?">
    MoEngage's Merlin AI Studio features—specifically Copywriter, Designer, Jinja Generator, and Segmentation Assist—undergo internal vulnerability assessment and penetration testing (VAPT) targeting prompt injection and jailbreak attempts. Our defense-in-depth controls include real-time input/output filtering, prompt shields, anomaly detection, threat modeling for indirect attacks, role-based access control (RBAC) and audit logging to prevent tool misuse, data loss prevention (DLP) controls against data exfiltration, and toxicity/brand-guideline checks paired with mandatory human-in-the-loop approval to limit model abuse.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="7.6 How often do you conduct vulnerability scans and penetration testing?">
    MoEngage performs quarterly vulnerability scans and internal penetration tests across our core platform, supplemented by annual external penetration tests conducted by independent third parties, with follow-up verification scans after remediation. Within Merlin AI Studio, internal AI-specific penetration testing aligned with the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs has been conducted for Copywriter, Designer, Jinja Generator, and Segmentation Assist, and we are actively extending this testing scope to additional AI features alongside ongoing robustness monitoring for bias, anomalies, and adversarial resilience.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="7.7 Do you have a documented Secure Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)?">
    Yes, based on the CERT DevSecOps model, including risk assessment and threat modeling, secure coding aligned with OWASP Top 10 / SANS Top 25, automated SAST and DAST, software composition analysis (SCA), and application-layer penetration testing. For AI/GenAI models specifically, our SDLC covers model validation, continuous performance/bias monitoring, and retraining policies aligned with the NIST AI RMF and OWASP Top 10 for LLMs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="7.8 How is model drift monitored, and how often are models retrained?">
    Model performance (accuracy, precision, recall, F-score) is monitored continuously via automated, threshold-based alerts, complemented by human validation. Retraining is triggered when thresholds are breached, or anomalies are detected; timing ranges from daily incremental updates (processing the last 24 hours of data) to a full retrain taking weeks to months, depending on complexity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 8. External Connectors

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="8.1 What are External Connectors, and what third-party applications do Custom Agents support?">
    External Connectors allow Custom Agents to integrate with your existing enterprise application stack—enabling an agent to read context from and trigger actions within those external tools during an execution run.

    Supported third-party integrations include Google Workspace, Slack, Atlassian (Jira/Confluence), and Salesforce Marketing Cloud, with additional enterprise platforms being integrated continuously.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="8.2 How is access authenticated and governed when an agent connects to an external tool?">
    Access operates strictly on an individual, user-delegated OAuth model:

    - **Individual Authentication (No Shared Service Accounts):** Each user connects their own third-party account via standard OAuth. Agents never rely on shared service accounts or system-wide credentials.
    - **Least-Privilege Execution:** When an agent acts through an external connector, it operates solely within the authenticated user's existing permissions in that third-party application. It cannot view, access, or modify anything beyond what that specific user is authorized to see.
    - **Per-Tool Action Controls:** Each connector allows granular control over specific capabilities, enabling administrators or users to disable individual actions. If an agent attempts to execute an action that has been toggled off, the execution is automatically blocked.
    - **Revocation & Deprovisioning:** Connector authorizations can be revoked at any point by the user who initiated the connection, by a workspace administrator, or automatically via standard enterprise identity deprovisioning workflows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="8.3 Where does data flow when an External Connector is enabled, and how is it governed under our contract?">
    Enabling a connector authorizes the agent to transfer data bi-directionally between MoEngage and your connected third-party platform to execute requested tasks.

    - **Data Residency & Boundary:** Any data retrieved from or written into an external application enters that provider's environment directly.
    - **Contractual Governance:** Data residing in or written to connected applications is governed exclusively by your organization's direct licensing and Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with that specific third-party vendor, rather than by MoEngage's DPA.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 9. Data Residency and Sub-Processors

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="9.1 Where is our data stored and processed?">

## 10. Monitoring, Incident Response and Business Continuity

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="10.1 How are AI activities logged, and can audit trails be produced for regulators?">
    Yes. MoEngage maintains audit trails covering user actions, timestamps, IP addresses, and outcomes, supported by centralized SIEM logging, version-controlled configurations, and change logs. Comprehensive, exportable audit logs and compliance evidence packs are available via the [MoEngage Trust Portal](https://trust.moengage.com/) for regulatory review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="10.2 What incident response processes are in place for AI-related events?">
    MoEngage maintains a formal Security Incident Response Plan (SIRP) aligned with NIST 800-61, covering detection, analysis, containment, remediation, recovery, and post-incident review, with defined severity levels and escalation protocols. AI-related threats are managed through the same Security Incident Response Team (SIRT) process, with the ability to immediately disable or roll back AI features if required. The full plan is tested at least annually via tabletop exercises.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="10.3 Is there a &quot;kill switch&quot; for AI features?">
    Yes. MoEngage supports immediate disabling of AI features and automations via per-workspace feature flags and the ability to pause or stop individual flows. There is currently no single global switch that halts all campaigns and channels simultaneously across a workspace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="10.4 What business continuity and disaster recovery capabilities exist?">
    MoEngage maintains ISO 22301-certified business resiliency plans, reviewed and approved annually, with a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of 6 hours and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of 12 hours for critical services. Our multi-region, multi-availability-zone AWS architecture (active-active, n+1) is tested annually through tabletop exercises, application recovery tests, and structured walkthroughs, with encrypted backups stored offsite in geographically separate locations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
