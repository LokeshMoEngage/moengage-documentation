# Introduction

Merlin AI Segment Assist lets you create segments by describing the audience you want in plain English. Instead of finding the right events and combining filters yourself, you type a prompt, and Segment Assist maps your request to the matching user and event filters, previews the resulting audience, and lets you refine the query through a conversation before you save it.

For AI-assisted segment creation through an external assistant like Claude or ChatGPT instead of the MoEngage dashboard, see the [MoEngage MCP segmentation tools](/user-guide/ai-and-intelligence/merlin-ai/moengage-mcp-server#segments).

## How Segment Assist Works

Segment Assist turns your plain-English description of an audience into a segment you can preview, edit, and save. It works from the events and user attributes that already exist in your workspace, so it maps your request to your own data rather than to a fixed list of terms. Because of this, the same description can produce a different segment in a different workspace.

When you enter a prompt, Segment Assist:

1. Reads your description and identifies the conditions in it, such as user attributes (for example, city or operating system) and events (for example, a purchase in the last 7 days).
2. Maps each condition to a matching event or attribute in your workspace, and checks that the values you mentioned exist for those fields.
3. Builds a query from the mapped conditions and validates it.
4. Shows the result as an editable segment, along with an explanation of the choices it made, such as the events it selected and the time window it applied.

You then refine the query with follow-up prompts or the suggested follow-up actions, preview the audience, and save it as a segment.

### Segment Cards

Each time you preview an audience or create a segment, Segment Assist adds a **segment card** to the chat, labeled Segmentation query 1, Segmentation query 2, and so on. A card represents one version of your query. Click a card to open its filters in the panel on the right, where you can review or edit them.

When a chat contains more than one card, use the dropdown at the top of the chat to switch between them.

### Refine Across Turns

Segment Assist keeps the context of your conversation, so you can build a segment over several turns. Each follow-up request refines the current query and produces a new card, and you can switch back to an earlier card at any time. For example, you can ask Segment Assist to add a condition, change a time window, or remove a filter, and it rebuilds the query from the current version.

### Clarification and Human-in-the-Loop

When Segment Assist cannot confidently map your request, it pauses and asks you to clarify before it builds the segment. It shows the question in the chat with a set of options to choose from, and can also let you add your own option. Segment Assist continues once you make a selection. Clarification can happen when you first describe a segment and when you refine one.

Clarification happens when:

- **Ambiguous term**: If a phrase such as "active users" could mean several things in your workspace, Segment Assist shows the possible options and asks you to choose.
- **Unrecognized value**: If a value isn't found for a field, Segment Assist shows the values that the field actually has.
- **Missing detail**: If a condition needs a number that you didn't provide, Segment Assist asks for it.

![Segment assist clarification card asking which of two conflicting conditions to keep, with a dropdown of options to choose from.](/images/segment-assist-clarification.webp)

If your workspace has no event or attribute that can express part of your request, Segment Assist tells you instead of substituting a different field.

### Impossible or Empty Requests

Some requests can't produce a meaningful audience. For example, "users who live in Bangalore and have never been to India" combines two conditions that can't both be true. Instead of building a segment that matches no users, Segment Assist asks which condition you want to keep.

When a valid query simply matches no users, Segment Assist explains the likely reason and offers ways to widen the audience, such as extending the time window, removing the time window, or creating the segment anyway.

<Note>
  Segment Assist can make mistakes. Review the generated query and preview the audience before you create the segment.
</Note>

# Create a Segment with Segment Assist

Perform the following steps to create a segment with Segment Assist:

1. On the sidebar menu in MoEngage, hover over **Segment** and click **+ New Segment**. The **Create segment** page appears.
2. In the top-right corner, click **Use MerlinAI to create segments**. The **Segment assist** page appears.

   ![Segment assist page showing suggested segment-type chips, the prompt box, and the Say hello to Segment Assist panel.](/images/segment-assist-landing.png)
3. In the **Describe the segment you want to build** box, enter a prompt and submit it. For example, `Build a segment of users who abandoned their cart`.

   To get started quickly, you can select one of the suggested segment types under **What would you like to see today?**, such as **Active users**, **Abandoned cart users**, or **Behavioral segment**. For guidance on writing prompts, see [Guidelines to Write Effective Prompts](#guidelines-to-write-effective-prompts).

   Segment Assist looks up your user attributes and events and generates a query.

   ![Segment assist chat showing a submitted prompt and the Looking up your user attributes and events loading state.](/images/segment-assist-prompt-loading.png)
4. Review the generated query and Segment Assist's explanation of the choices it made, such as the events it mapped your request to and the time window it applied. Confirm the query matches your intent.

   ![Segment assist response explaining the query choices, with a Follow-ups list including Preview audience, Create segment, Use last 90 days instead, and Edit conditions.](/images/segment-assist-query-followups.png)
5. To adjust the query or move forward, select one of the suggested **Follow-ups** below the response. The available options change based on the query, and can include:
   - **Preview audience**: Check the audience the query matches.
   - **Use last 90 days instead**: Widen the time window.
   - **Edit conditions**: Open the query in the filter view to edit it manually. For more information, see [Edit the Query in Filter View](#edit-the-query-in-filter-view).
   - **Create segment**: Save the query as a segment.

   You can also type your own follow-up prompt instead of selecting a suggestion. The follow-up suggestions and any clarification prompts Segment Assist shows are dynamic, so they vary from one prompt or follow-up to the next.
6. To check the audience before you save it, click **Preview audience**. The preview shows the **User count**, **Reachable users** (with the reachable percentage), a set of **Sample users**, and **Reachability by channel** across Push, Email, SMS, and WhatsApp.
   - When you run a **Preview audience** or **Create segment** action, Segment Assist adds a segment card for that query to the chat. Click a card in the chat to open its filters in the panel on the right.
   - If the query matches no users, the preview shows an empty segment. Refine the query with a follow-up prompt, for example by widening the time window.
   - Some fields, such as `email` and `mobile_number`, may be masked in the preview based on your role's permissions. Segment Assist tells you which fields are masked.

   ![Audience preview showing User count 16, Reachable users 16 at 100 percent, sample users, and reachability by channel across Push, Email, SMS, and WhatsApp.](/images/segment-assist-audience-preview.png)
7. To refine the audience, enter a follow-up prompt in the chat. For example, `Rebuild and preview this segment using a last 90 days window instead of 30 days`. Each refinement produces a new segment card, such as Segmentation query 1 and Segmentation query 2.
   - To move between the cards in a chat, use the dropdown at the top of the chat, or click a card in the conversation. Click a card to view its filters in the panel on the right.
   - To start over with a different audience, click **New chat**. Your past conversations are available under **History**, scoped to your user in the current workspace.

   ![Segment assist with the query selector dropdown open, listing the segment cards in the chat, and a card's filters shown in the panel on the right.](/images/segment-assist-query-selector.png)
8. When the preview matches what you want, click **Create custom segment** to save the audience as a segment. You can also click **Create segment** from the follow-up actions. Segment Assist generates a name for the segment. To use a specific name, include it in your prompt before you run the create segment action.

## Edit the Query in Filter View

To adjust the generated query manually, click **Edit filters** above the audience preview. The filter view opens the query as editable filters, where you can:

- Change the events, attributes, and conditions (for example, **Has executed** or **Has not executed**).
- Add nested filters within a condition.
- Combine conditions with **AND** or **OR**.
- Add more filters, or turn on **Exclude Users**.

Click **Update filters** to apply your changes, or **Cancel** to discard them. For more information on building segments manually, see [Rule-based filter segments](/user-guide/segment/create-segments/rule-based-filter-segments).

![Edit filters view showing editable conditions combined with AND, with Nested Filter, Filter, and Exclude Users options.](/images/segment-assist-edit-filters.png)

# Guidelines to Write Effective Prompts

The following guidelines and prompt structures help Segment Assist generate accurate queries.

<Info>
  - Review the [key concepts](/user-guide/segment/getting-started/basics-of-segmentation) of segmentation at MoEngage before writing prompts.
  - User attributes and user activity (events and event attributes) are the building blocks of segmentation.
  - Filters narrow results to specific user attributes and events.
  - Operators (AND/OR) combine multiple conditions.
</Info>

## Prompt Structures

Use the following structures to describe common segments:

- Query events (user behavior) and user attributes (user properties) clearly so Segment Assist maps each to the right filter.
  - **User attributes:** Segment users whose city, country, email ID, or mobile number is `<value>`. For example, "Segment users who are in New York City."
  - **Events:** Segment users who have done or performed an `<event>` in the last n days. For example, "Segment users who opened the app in the last 7 days."
  - **Events with event attributes:** Segment users who have performed an `<event>` with `<event attribute>` containing `<value>`. For example, "Segment users who updated the app in the last 15 days with app versions containing 2.3."
  - **Users who have not performed an event:** Segment users who have not performed an `<event>` in the last n days. For example, "Segment users who have not updated the app in the last 15 days."
- Combine user attributes and events with AND or OR as your business logic requires. State the user attribute first, followed by the event. For example, "Segment users who are in New York City and who opened the app in the last 30 days."
- For object or array-of-object user properties and event attributes, write the full nested path using the exact field names, separated by dots. For example, if a `nations` object property contains a `cities` field that contains a `name` field, write "Segment users whose nations.cities.name is Chennai."

## Sample Prompts to Get You Started

Use these sample prompts as a reference for structure and phrasing. Copy a prompt, then paste it into the **Describe the segment you want to build** box.

<Prompt description="7-day active users">
  Segment users who opened the app in the last 7 days.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Users from a city">
  Segment users who are in New York City.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Perform an action">
  Segment users who opened an email last week.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Number of actions">
  Segment users who have clicked a push notification at least 5 times in the last 30 days.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Joint conditions">
  Segment users who are in New York City and who opened the app in the last 30 days.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Did not perform an action">
  Segment users who have not updated the app in the last 15 days.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Channel interactions">
  Segment users who have clicked a notification from an Android device at least once in the last 3 days.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="For the first time">
  Segment users who opened the app for the first time in the last 30 days.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Data validation">
  Segment users whose email IDs are not available.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Starts with">
  Segment users whose mobile number starts with +91.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Ends with">
  Segment users whose email ID ends with education.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Contains a substring">
  Segment users whose email ID contains @moengage.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Contains multiple substrings">
  Segment users whose email ID contains @moengage, @gmail, @hotmail.
</Prompt>

<Prompt description="Numerical actions">
  Segment users who opened the app in the last 3 days with an app version greater than 16.1.
</Prompt>

<Info>
  - Segment Assist creates all segments in the default team. It doesn't support creating a segment for a specific team.
  - Segment Assist creates all segments in the default app. It doesn't support creating a segment for a specific app in a multi-app workspace.
  - Segment Assist can access a blacklisted attribute in data management for 1 day from the day of blacklisting. The attribute is excluded from your results from the next day. Check your results before proceeding.
</Info>
