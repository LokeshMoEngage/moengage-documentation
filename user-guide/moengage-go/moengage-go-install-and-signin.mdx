This page describes what you need before you start, how to install the app, and how to sign in for the first time.

## Prerequisites

To use the app, you need:

- An existing MoEngage account with access to the web dashboard. The app is for users who already use MoEngage on the web; you sign in with the same account and workspace. There is no separate sign-up in the app.
- Permission to view the campaigns you want to monitor. The app shows the same campaigns and data your account can access on the web.
- If 2-step verification is enabled for your account, it must be set up on your device. For more information, refer to [Set up 2-step verification](#set-up-2-step-verification).
- If you approve campaigns, the campaign approval flow must be enabled for your team on the web dashboard.

## Sign in

Sign in to the app with your existing MoEngage account. After you enter your email, the app takes you to the workspace you were invited to or used last. To sign in to another workspace you have access to, tap **Login to a different workspace**. The sign-in options shown depend on the login methods the workspace allows: password, Google, or SSO.

### Sign in to your last-used workspace

1. On the **Login to your workspace** screen, type your email address in the **Work email** box and tap **Continue**.
   <img alt="Login to your workspace screen with the Work email box and Continue button" src="/images/moe-go-login-1aa.png" width="50%" />
2. On the **Login to your accounts** screen, confirm the email address shown under **You will be logged in as**, and sign in using a method available for the workspace:
   * **Password**: Type your password in the **Password** box and tap **Login**.
   * **Google**: Tap **Google** and choose your Google account.
   * **SSO**: Tap **SSO** and authenticate with your organization's identity provider in the browser. After you authenticate, you are returned to the app.

   <img alt="Login to your accounts screen with the Password box, Login button, and Google and SSO options" src="/images/moe-go-login-1b.png" width="50%" />
3. If 2-step verification is enabled for your account, a verification screen opens. Enter the code. For more information, refer to [Verify your identity](#verify-your-identity).
   If 2-step verification is not enabled for your account, this step is skipped.
4. After you sign in, the app opens to the **Campaigns** screen for the workspace.

### Sign in to a different workspace

1. On the **Login to your workspace** screen, type your email address in the **Work email** box and tap **Login to a different workspace**.
   <img alt="Login to your workspace screen with the Login to a different workspace link highlighted" src="/images/moe-go-login-2a.png" width="50%" />
2. If 2-step verification is enabled for your account, a verification screen opens. Enter the code. For more information, refer to [Verify your identity](#verify-your-identity).
   If 2-step verification is not enabled for your account, this step is skipped.

   <Note>
     If you have access to multiple workspaces, enter the code from your authenticator app entry for the workspace you last used.
   </Note>
3. On the **Select workspace** screen, tap the **Workspace** box and choose the workspace you want to sign in to. The workspace you used most recently is labeled **(last used)**. The login methods available for the selected workspace are shown below the box.
      <img alt="Select workspace screen with the Workspace list, login methods, Password box, and Continue with Google button" src="/images/moe-go-login-3a.png" width="50%" />
4. Sign in using a method available for the workspace:
   * **Password**: Type your password in the **Password** box and tap **Login**.
   * **Google**: Tap **Continue with Google** and choose your Google account.
   * **SSO**: Tap **Continue with SSO** and authenticate with your organization's identity provider in the browser. After you authenticate, you are returned to the app.
5. After you sign in, the app opens to the **Campaigns** screen for the workspace you selected.

<Note>
  * If you select a different workspace after typing your password, the password is cleared. Type it again for the new workspace.
  * In workspaces that use SSO, admins also see a **Password** box. Password login is available to admins so they are not locked out if there are SSO configuration issues.
  * To return to the previous screen, tap **Back**.
</Note>

### Verify your identity

If 2-step verification is enabled for your account, a verification screen opens during sign-in. If 2-step verification is enabled but not yet set up, the **Set up device authentication** screen opens instead. For more information, refer to [Set up 2-step verification](#set-up-2-step-verification).

You verify with either an authenticator app OTP or an email OTP. The identity and access management (IAM) settings for your account decide which one you get; you cannot choose.

* **Authenticator app OTP**: On the **User authentication** screen, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app in the **Verification code** boxes.

  <img alt="User authentication screen with the Verification code boxes and the Verifying status" src="/images/authy.png" width="50%" />
* **Email OTP**: On the **Email OTP verification** screen, type the 6-digit OTP sent to your email in the **Enter OTP** boxes. The screen shows a countdown timer. To get a new code, tap **Resend OTP** at any time.

  <img alt="Email OTP verification screen with the Enter OTP boxes, countdown timer, and Resend OTP link" src="/images/email-otp.png" width="50%" />

After you type the last digit, the app shows **Verifying...** and checks the code.

### Set up 2-step verification

If a workspace requires 2-step verification and you have not set it up yet, the **Set up device authentication** screen opens at the verification step of sign-in. The app sends a setup link to your email, and the link opens the app to complete the setup.

1. On the **Set up device authentication** screen, confirm your email address in the **Work email** box and tap **Send link**.
   <img alt="Set up device authentication screen with the Work email box and Send link button" src="/images/moe-go-2fa-send-link.png" width="50%" />
2. On your mobile device, open the setup email and tap the link. The MoEngage Go app opens the **Set up device authentication** screen with a secret key.
3. Download and install an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator or Authy.
4. Open the authenticator app, add a new account, and enter the secret key. To copy the key, tap the copy icon next to it.
5. Type the 6-digit verification code from the authenticator app in the **Verification code** boxes. The app verifies the code after you enter the last digit.
   <img alt="Set up device authentication screen with the secret key, copy icon, and Verification code boxes" src="/images/moe-go-2fa-setup.png" width="50%" />
6. When the **Device has been successfully set up** message appears, tap **Download recovery codes** and store the codes somewhere safe.
   <img alt="Device has been successfully set up screen with the Login button and Download recovery codes link" src="/images/moe-go-2fa-success.png" width="50%" />
7. Tap **Login**. The **Login to your workspace** screen opens. Sign in again with your email.

<Note>
  To return to the email screen without sending a setup link, tap **Back**.
</Note>

### Workspaces you cannot sign in to from the app

If a workspace is not available on the app, a message appears when you try to sign in to it:

* On the **Login to your accounts** screen, an alert appears for your last-used workspace. To sign in to another workspace, tap **Login to a different workspace**.
* On the **Select workspace** screen, the message appears in place of the sign-in options. Select another workspace.

You can also sign in to the workspace on the web dashboard.

| Workspace | Message shown |
| - | - |
| Blocked workspace | This workspace is blocked. Contact your customer success manager. |
| Test environment workspace | Test environment access for this workspace is limited to the web. Select another workspace to proceed or continue on the web. |

### Facing login issues?

If you can't sign in, tap **Facing login issues?** at the bottom of the sign-in screen.

* **Password reset**: Password reset is not available in the app. To reset your password, use the MoEngage dashboard in a desktop browser.
* **Other help**: Contact your customer success manager (CSM) or email [pm@moengage.com](mailto:pm@moengage.com).

## Enable push notifications

To receive notifications on your device, allow notifications when the app prompts you. If you skip this step, you can enable notifications later in your device settings for the app. Without notifications enabled, they still appear in the Notifications tab, but you will not be notified when they arrive.
