# Configure ICS

ICS is a mobile engagement and communications provider enabling enterprises to intelligently personalize their two-way marketing and customer care interactions across multiple channels. MoEngage offers a native integration with ICS for WhatsApp, allowing businesses to send templated WhatsApp messages using Senders powered by ICS.

## Sender Configuration

To configure a Sender from ICS on the MoEngage Dashboard, do the following:

1. Go to Settings->WhatsApp->Sender Configuration.
2. Click +Add connector on the left menu.
3. Choose ICS from the list of WA providers or connectors available in the dropdown and click *Done*.
